Generation 9 Resource Pack follower sprites: credit the artists

The walking sprites KaizoCore draws for the Pokemon PMD Sprite Collab has not drawn,
and DarkusShadow's own posts do not cover (walkingpals-expo), are the follower
sheets from Caruban's Generation 9 Resource Pack, version 3.3.8:

  https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1101/

The pack's page says to give the artists credit. Caruban compiles the pack.
The artists, as that page lists them for the overworlds used here:

  Gen 5 (Simisear, Simipour, Tranquill, Blitzle, Zebstrika, Throh, Crustle,
  Tirtouga, Carracosta, Amoonguss, Frillish, Shelmet, Bouffalant):
  MissingLukey, help-14, Kymoyonian, cSc-A7X, 2and2makes5, Pokegirl4ever,
  Fernandojl, Silver-Skies, TyranitarDark, Getsuei-H, Kid1513, Milomilotic11,
  Kyt666, kdiamo11, Chocosrawlooid, Syledude, Gallanty, Gizamimi-Pichu, Zyon17,
  LarryTurbo, spritesstealer.

  Gen 7 (Trumbeak, Gumshoos, Shiinotic, Oranguru): Larry Turbo, princess-phoenix.

  Gen 8 (Rolycoly, Carkol, Coalossal, Mr. Rime, Falinks, Cufant, Zarude):
  SageDeoxys, Wolfang62, LarryTurbo, tammyclaydon.

  Maschiff: Carmanekko.
  Iron Jugulis: TooManyLuigis, Mortedesu, Billla and Wolfang62.

Mega Falinks walks as Falinks until it has a sheet of its own. Each sheet's
artists are also in walkingpals-expo/credits.tsv. A Sprite Collab sheet, when
one is drawn, replaces these.
