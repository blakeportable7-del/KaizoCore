DarkusShadow's overworld sprites: free use with credit

The walking sprites KaizoCore draws for Pokemon that PMD Sprite Collab has not drawn
(walkingpals-darkus) are DarkusShadow's overworld sprites, from his DeviantArt gallery,
deviantart.com/darkusshadow. His terms, as his posts and his "Gen 9 (Paldea) Pokemon
Overworld Sprites" sheet state them:

  "Free Use. Give Credits If Used."
  "This is free for everyone to use in their Pokemon projects! Please give credits if used!"

Credits, as his credits list and each post name them:

  DarkusShadow: Squawkabilly, Mabosstiff, Shroodle, Brambleghast, Toedscruel, Klawf,
  Rabsca, Espathra, Bombirdier, Flamigo, Gimmighoul, Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Miraidon,
  Okidogi, Iron Boulder and Iron Crown.
  Brute Bonnet: original sprite by TyranitarDark, by DarkusShadow.
  Mega Malamar: original sprite by princess-phoenix, by DarkusShadow ("Give credits to
  both if used").

Each sheet's post is listed in the app's assets, walkingpals-darkus/credits.tsv.
