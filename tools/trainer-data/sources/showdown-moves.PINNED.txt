A build input only. convert_natdex_move_desc.py READS it as data (nothing in it is run) for the
move descriptions of the Nat. Dex and MaxDex moves that neither the Nat. Dex Extension nor the DS
tracker describes: the moves from X and Y on. It takes each move's shortDesc, never its desc. The
app does not carry this file; it carries those descriptions, in
tracker-gba/src/main/resources/natdex/movedesc.tsv, and Showdown's license ships with them
(app/src/main/assets/licenses/MIT-PokemonShowdown.txt, NOTICE).

To move to a newer Showdown: fetch data/text/moves.ts at the new commit, write its commit, date,
size and SHA-256 here and its SHA-256 in convert_natdex_move_desc.py, rerun the script, and read
the diff of movedesc.tsv.
