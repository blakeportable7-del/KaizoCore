## HOW TO USE
If you don't have the required Randomizer ZX program and Java yet, [download them here](https://gist.github.com/UTDZac/a147c497424dfbd537d8c4b0c22b5621#requirements-for-use).

Setting strings are a convenient way to share Randomizer settings with other people. By using these setting strings, you'll ensure that you get the exact same Randomizer settings as everyone else. This helps make sure that your ROM(s) are properly setup and randomized to fit the IronMon ruleset.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/4258818/223565848-d8826ae0-f9cf-42d9-b5a2-864508827081.png)

To use these setting strings:

1. Open the Randomizer ZX program through the `..._launcher.bat` file
2. Click **"Open ROM"** and select your Pokémon game ROM
3. Click "Settings" then **"Get/Load Settings String"**
4. In the bottom text field, paste (`Ctrl + V`) the **settings string** for your game and the IronMon challenge mode you want to play
   - The setting strings for all games and challenge modes can be found below on this page
5. Click OK
6. Click **"Randomize (Save)"** in the upper-right
7. Type in a name for your ROM and click **"Save"**
8. Click **"Yes"** on the prompt to Save the log file
   - You don't have to, nor should you, look inside the log file until the game is over
   - However, if you choose "No," you won't be able to get the log later
9. You're done and ready to play!

How-to-use [visual image guide](https://imgur.com/a/owJeSZo) and walkthrough.

## KAIZO DOUBLES RULES
Kaizo doubles is a fun variant of Kaizo Ironmon where every battle is a doubles battle (2v2). These rules are still a work in progress, so check back often for updates.

1) Double Battles only! All trainer battles possible are doubles battles (2v2). Thus, if either of your two Pokémon faint it's game over and the run ends.
2) You may pivot twice per route instead of once. This includes pivoting to the same Pokémon a second time.
3) Both of your Pokémon must be different. If evolving a Pokémon results in having two of the same, that's allowed and bypasses this rule.
4) If a move is shared between both your Pokémon, you cannot use that move at all for either of them.
   - For example, if both Pokémon have Water Spout, then neither Pokémon can use the move Water Spout until one of them forgets it.
5) The move Dark Void is banned for double battles.
6) Randomizer Settings Changes
   - Standardize EXP Curves to: Medium Slow
   - Trainer Pokémon: Double Battle Mode (enabled)
   - Additional Pokémon for: Important Trainers +1, Regular Trainers +1, (Keep Boss Trainers +3)

## RED / BLUE / YELLOW

#### Rules updates for Standard & up
1) It is recommended that you play Pokémon Yellow instead of Red or Blue. The main reason for this is that Brock is a bit easier in Yellow, but future gyms will be a bit tougher. This is ensures an appropriate level of difficulty.
2) You are allowed to buy one Fresh Water but only to give it to the guard to enter Saffron City. You cannot use it for healing.
3) If you randomly get a drink item to get into Saffron City earlier, you may do so but with some restrictions:
   - You may collect items and fight non-story related trainers
   - You may NOT buy a drink to replace the item you turned in
   - You may NOT skip Rock Tunnel or otherwise enter Rocket Game Corner, Lavender Town, Celadon Gym
4) The randomizer setting for "Force Change" an evolution is disabled.  This is to add a bit more variety in evos, and to stop Dratini from directly evolving into Dragonite.
5) Unlike other Pokémon games, Gen 1 does not have a fluctuating exp curve option. As such, two separate randomizer settings files and "randomizations" are required to produce a proper Ironmon game. The main reason this is necessary is to prevent your Pokémon from evolving into legendary Pokémon.
6) For more details on rules updates for Gen 1 Kaizo, please see the associated [PasteBin](https://pastebin.com/XgD2iwLc)

#### Rules updates for Kaizo & up
1) All Pokémon greater than 480 BST are banned. This includes: Dragonite, Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mew, and Mewtwo.
2) The following moves are banned: Wrap, Bind, Fire Spin, and Clamp
3) Allowed [glitches](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_glitches_(Generation_I)): Bide errors, Counter glitches, Hyper Beam + Freeze/Sleep, Invulnerability glitch, Substitute + Confusion glitch, Evolution stone bypassing

#### Rules updates for Survival
1) New rule "Evolutionary Stone Favors" replaces existing rule "Flute Favors"
   - As there are no flutes, you can instead collect and trade in the five evo stones: Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, and Moon
2) New rule "HM 01, Buddy Has Fun" replaces "Cut a Move, Hold Any Item"
   - As there are no held items, teaching Cut to your main Pokémon allows you to use your HM friend to compensate.
   - In a Gym or Elite 4, while outside of battle, you may swap your HM friend to your lead party slot before going into battle.
   - This affords you a free turn of item usage, no restrictions. After using the item, simply throw Pokéballs until your HM friend faints, or manually swap back to your main Pokémon.
   - If you employ this strat, you must swap your HM friend to something caught in route 1 to minimize its level.
   - Once your HM friend faints in this manner, you cannot revive it until you spend one of your Pokécenter heals.
3) If your Pokémon has 351 BST or lower AND it's still capable of an evolution, then the following moves are legal for it to use: Wrap, Bind, Fire Spin, Clamp.
4) Allowed [glitches](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_glitches_(Generation_I)): Toxic counter glitches - with Leech Seed or Rest

## X / Y

#### Rules updates for Standard & up
1) **New Rule: Healing Item Rebalance**
   - After beating a gym, you can buy 1 Super Potion.
   - You can trade-up by selling exactly 4 Super Potions to buy 1 Hyper Potion. You can make this trade any number of times.
2) The Rock Smash TM is allowed for story progression but you must treat it as an "HM" move.
3) Super Training and O-Powers are banned.
4) Berry trees are allowed. Note: Only 1 berry can be obtained from each berry tree.
5) Battle Chateau (Dungeon): You can only fight a maximum of 6 trainers inside this building.
6) Sky battles are allowed but only if your Pokémon is eligible for them (Flying-type or has Levitate).
7) New Randomizer Settings for EXP/game balance:
   - Standardize EXP Curve: Fluctuating -> Medium Slow
   - Foe Pokémon: Trainer Percentage Level Modifer: 50% -> 42%
   - TM/HMs: Keep Field Move TMs: Should be enabled

#### Rules updates for Ultimate & up
1) Connecting Cave is not a dungeon.
2) Both parts of Lost Hotel make up a single dungeon; you can't heal inbetween until you decide to fully leave.
3) All parts of Victory Road (inside and out) are considered a single dungeon. Once you step foot in the cave, you cannot leave until you reach the Pokémon League.
   - Kaizo players can't use the healing NPC near the rival battle.

#### Rules updates for Kaizo & Survival
1. No using 601+ BST Pokémon. Megas, Pseudos, Mythicals, and Legendaries under this limit are allowed, and available in the grass.
   - The inclusion of Legendaries is a recent change.
2. The abilities Huge/Pure Power are banned except for Pokémon with BST 450 or lower.
3. The abilities Parental Bond, Fur Coat, and Protean are banned except for Pokémon with BST 525 or lower.
4. Poison Heal is okay but you can't purposefully get poisoned from wild Pokémon.
5. Banned abilities on your starter Pokémon can be used for the first two mandatory fights (in XY: the initial Rival fight and the first Youngster).
   - For Survival: Due to pivot limitations, healing at the PC doesn't count against you until you fight the second Trainer

#### Settings Strings
**NOTE: There is a bug with v4.6.0 of the Randomizer: You need to manually turn on two settings.** Click "Starters, Statics & Trades", then enable the "Randomize Starter Held Items" and "Ban Bad Items" underneath it.

Every time you load settings, you will *always* have to manually re-apply these two settings. They simply do not load properly.
