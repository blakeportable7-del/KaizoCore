### Changes to Kaizo Rules for the [Randomizer ZX](https://github.com/Ajarmar/universal-pokemon-randomizer-zx/releases/latest):
- **Pokémon Types:** Random (follow evolutions)
- **Move Data:** Randomize move: Power, Accuracy, and PP
- **Pokémon Abilities:** Ensure Two Abilities
- **Pokémon Evolutions:** Make Evolutions Easier
   - This results in level 40 being the latest a Pokémon will evolve. Middle-stage evos occur no later than level 30. Friendship evos occur at 160 happiness instead of 220; most Pokémon start at 70 friendship
   - _NOTE: Currently the Tracker doesn't reflect this change in levels, but the game will handle it correctly_
- **Trainer Pokémon:** Add Held Items to: Boss, Important, and Regular Trainers
- **Trainer Pokémon:** Pokemon League Has Unique Pokémon: 1
- **TMs & HMs:** Full Compatibility

### New Rules to Obey:
- **Held Items Restriction:** All items are allowed to be held, except for the ones listed below under Banned Items
- **Head Start:** If your main Pokémon has **405 or less BST**, you may defeat up to three Pokémon before fighting the first non-rival Trainer
- **Abilities:** Any previously banned abilities are allowed only if:
   - Your main Pokémon hasn't fully evolved yet, and/or
   - Your main Pokémon evolved into something new that has a banned ability
- **TMs/HMs:** Any TM/HM found or acquired can be used on your main Pokémon, but only immediately after defeating a Gym leader. You can only use **one TM/HM per victory.**
   - An exception to the rule is that you can still use a TM on your starter Pokémon before the first battle
   - You may delete an HM move with the Move Deleter but you cannot teach the same Pokémon that HM ever again
   - Remember, you still cannot buy TMs in any way
   - This only applies to your main Pokémon, you can still teach HMs to your HM friend at any time

### Special Rules for Gyms
- **Items are Locked:** You cannot use items outside of battle in any way until you beat the Gym leader. This includes:
   - You cannot give or remove a held item from your Pokémon, including berries, herbs, etc.
   - You cannot use anything from your Item Bag: TMs, PP Ups, Rare Candies, etc.
- **Get Badges ASAP:** While in a city that has a Gym, **you must complete that Gym before leaving the city**. If there are other tasks or story requirements preventing you from doing so, then you must complete those tasks first then return and battle the Gym right away.
   - *Example:* You enter Vermillion City but are required to go to S.S. Anne for Cut; do that first, then do the gym
   - *Example:* Viridian City Gym is blocked until you get 7 badges; you must immediately go to back to this city and do this Gym once you obtain your 7th badge
   - *Editor's Note:* I'm interested in feedback about how this plays out for Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald
