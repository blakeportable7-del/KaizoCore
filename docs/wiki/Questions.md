# Questions

**Where do I get games?**
From your own cartridges. KaizoCore contains no games and downloads none, and a question in Issues about where to
find one gets no answer.

**The card says the tracker can't read my game.**
The tracker reads the US versions. Other languages, other versions and changed games still play, without the tracker.
Pokémon Black is not read yet: it needs one check against a real dump first.

**DS games are slow, or the app closes.**
DS games want a phone with 3 GB of RAM, and Black 2 and White 2 use about 1 GB on their own. Close other apps. DS
speed stops at 4x.

**Can I update without losing everything?**
Yes. Use Update in the app, or install the new APK over the old one. Never uninstall: that wipes your games, saves,
notes and attempts.

**Can I move to a new phone?**
Back up (More, Backup and info), copy the file to the new phone and restore it, then add your games again. Or use
cloud sync on both phones.

**Where did rewind and cheats go?**
They are off in Kaizo IronMON runs and Nuzlockes, and in RetroAchievements hardcore.

**Why is there no game-over popup in my Nuzlocke?**
It is a Kaizo IronMON thing. A Nuzlocke ends by its own rules.

**Do I need a capture card to stream?**
No. See [Streaming to OBS](Streaming-to-OBS).

**Is KaizoCore part of IronMON, or Nintendo?**
No. KaizoCore is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, The Pokémon Company, IronMON or RetroAchievements.

**Something broke.**
Let the app send the crash report, or open an [Issue](https://github.com/blakeportable7-del/KaizoCore/issues). Never
attach a ROM or a save.
