# Getting started

## 1. Install

Download the APK from [willowcreek.group/kaizocore](https://willowcreek.group/kaizocore) or the GitHub release, open
it, and let Android install it. [Install and update](Install-and-update) has the details.

You need Android 8 or newer. DS games want a phone with at least 3 GB of RAM.

## 2. Add your games

KaizoCore contains no games and downloads none. You need your own dumps.

1. Open **Library** in the bottom bar, then **My games**, then **Add files**.
2. Pick your files. `.gba`, `.gbc`, `.gb` and `.nds` all work, and so does a `.zip`: every game or patch inside it is
   added. `.7z` and `.rar` are not opened.
3. Each file gets a card that says what it is. A clean US copy of a game the tracker reads says **Tracker works** and
   sits under **Clean ROMs**. Anything else still plays, and its card says why the tracker can't read it.

## 3. Pick a mode

The **Home** screen has four buttons:

- **Kaizo IronMON**: a randomized game with the IronMON tracker. A loss means a new game. See [Kaizo IronMON](Kaizo-IronMON).
- **Nuzlocke**: catch the first Pokémon in each area. One that faints is gone. See [Nuzlocke](Nuzlocke).
- **Play any game**: anything in your library, with no rules and no randomizer. See [Playing any game](Playing-any-game).
- **ROM Hacks**: patch a fan-made hack onto a game you own, then play it. See [ROM hacks and patches](ROM-hacks-and-patches).

When a game is in progress, the **Continue** card at the top takes you straight back to it.

## 4. Your first Kaizo IronMON run

1. Home, **Kaizo IronMON**.
2. Pick the game, then the mode. The screen opens on Kaizo. The line under the modes says what that mode's rules
   add, and **Read all the rules** has the full text.
3. Tap **Start attempt 1**. The randomizer runs on the phone with the ruleset's own settings file, then the game boots.
4. Play. The tracker sits under the game in portrait and beside it in landscape, and fills in when you get your
   first Pokémon.

## Finding your way around

- **Home**: the four modes, Continue, and links to Library, More and Your stats.
- **Play**: the game. **File**, top right, holds save states, speed, rewind, cheats, streaming, the layout editor and
  the rest. **SETUP** on the tracker opens Tracker Setup.
- **Library**: **My games**, and **Patched versions**.
- **More**: **Controls** (keys and buttons), and **Backup and info** (backups, cloud sync, updates, bug reports).
