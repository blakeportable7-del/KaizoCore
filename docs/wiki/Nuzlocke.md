# Nuzlocke

A Nuzlocke is a challenge you set yourself: catch only the first Pokémon in each area, and a Pokémon that faints is
dead. KaizoCore's tracker keeps the ledger for you on every game it reads, Red to White 2.

## Start one

Home, **Nuzlocke**. Pick the game and a preset, then **Start Nuzlocke**. **Rules in detail** shows every switch.

**Presets**: Standard, Hardcore, Randomizer, Monotype, Wedlocke and Genlocke. Each is a starting point, and you can
change any switch in it.

## The 19 rules

- **The rules**: First encounter per area. A fainted Pokémon is dead. A whiteout ends the run. Slow start (the rules
  begin once you hold Poké Balls, so the first routes are free).
- **Clauses**: Dupes clause. Dupes: only lines you still own. Shiny clause. Gifts count. Static Pokémon count. Cave
  floors are one area. Water is its own area. Escape clause. Nickname reminder.
- **Hardcore**: Level caps. Rivals and team leaders are bosses. No items in battle. Set battle style.
- **Variants**: Wedlocke pairs. Genlocke.

There is also a choice for the Safari Zone: each zone its own area, or the whole Zone as one.

## What the tracker notices

- when the rules begin: once you hold Poké Balls on Gen 1 to 3, with your first Pokémon on DS
- your first encounter in each area, your catches and your gifts
- a Pokémon at 0 HP, and a warning if a dead one has HP again
- a whiteout, which ends the run if that rule is on
- beating the Champion, which completes the run
- level caps, items used in battle and Shift battle style (Gen 1 to 3)

It warns and it records. It never stops the game.

## Level caps

The cap is the level of the next boss's strongest Pokémon. Gym leaders are checked when the battle starts. The Elite
Four is checked once, when its first battle starts, against the strongest Pokémon in the whole Elite Four; inside the
League there is no cap after that, the Champion included. Rivals and villain team leaders count as bosses when
**Rivals and team leaders are bosses** is on.

## The ledger

**Open the ledger** on the Nuzlocke screen. It has five tabs: **AREAS**, **TEAM**, **GRAVE**, **LOG** and **RULES**.
The tracker fills it in as you play, and you can correct it: THIS DOES NOT COUNT, MARK DEAD, THE DEATH DOES NOT
COUNT, ADD A POKEMON, ADD A NOTE, change a rule (the change goes in the log), END THE RUN and OPEN IT AGAIN.

On the tracker, the Nuzlocke line shows the preset, how many are alive and dead, the area and the cap.

## Randomized Nuzlockes

The Randomizer preset randomizes the game first, and it starts from **Nuzlocke fair**: wild Pokémon and trainers'
teams are random but close in strength, and nothing else changes. Pick an IronMON mode instead if you want one. A
randomized Nuzlocke counts no attempt, and **NEW NUZLOCKE** in the File menu rolls a new seed with the same rules.

## During a Nuzlocke

Rewind and cheats are off. The game-over popup and A+B+Start are Kaizo IronMON things and do not appear: a Nuzlocke
ends by its own rules.

## On DS games

The DS tracker cannot see yet how a battle ended, ignores Slow start, does not track double battles, and does not
see a catch that goes straight to the box.
