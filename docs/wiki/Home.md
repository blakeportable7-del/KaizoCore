# KaizoCore

KaizoCore is an emulator for every Game Boy, Game Boy Advance and DS game you own, on Android. Pokémon games get
extra tools on top: Kaizo IronMON with a tracker that follows the PC trackers, Nuzlockes with a ledger that keeps itself, ROM hacks you patch onto
games you own, and plain play.

KaizoCore contains no games. You add game files you own, and they stay on your phone.
