# Playing any game

Everything in KaizoCore runs on a full emulator you can also just play. Home, **Play any game**, pick a game, **Play**.
No rules, no randomizer. Leave, and the game opens where you left it next time.

## Systems

Game Boy and Game Boy Color (Gambatte), Game Boy Advance (mGBA) and Nintendo DS (melonDS).

## The File menu

While you play, **File** (top right) opens: States, Save state, Load state, Rewind, Speed, Mute, Restart, Rules, Cam,
Stream, Clean view, Edit layout, Settings, Achievements, Cheats and Hide pad. In landscape the same things sit in a
strip beside the game.

## Save states

- 8 slots, each with a screenshot, plus the **Auto-save**.
- **Lock** a slot so it cannot be overwritten. **Undo** brings back the state a save overwrote.
- The auto-save is written every 3 minutes of play, at the start of a battle, and whenever you leave the game.
- Loading asks first ("Load slot 3?").

## Speed, slow motion and rewind

- **Speed**: 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x or 16x on Game Boy and GBA, and up to 4x on DS. The sound is off at any speed but 1x.
  Each game remembers its speed.
- **Slow motion**: 1/2x and 1/4x.
- **Rewind**: hold **Rewind** to go back up to about 30 seconds (12 on DS). It is off in Kaizo IronMON runs and
  Nuzlockes.

## Controls

- **Touch**: **Edit layout** lets you drag any button, tap it to size it (60 to 180%), set how see-through it is in
  landscape, pick a skin (Classic, Modern or Outline), or start from a preset: My Boy layout or Original pad on GBA and
  Game Boy, SuperNDS layout on DS. Each console keeps one layout for portrait and one for landscape.
- **Controllers**: a Bluetooth or USB controller works as it is, and the touch pad hides ("Controller connected. Pad
  hidden."). **Show pad anyway** brings it back.
- **Keyboard**: set your keys under More, **Controls**, **Keys and buttons**. These also go on a key or a pad button:
  Quick save, Quick load, Fast forward (hold), Open save states and Rewind (hold).
- **Rumble**: a game that rumbles buzzes the phone.

## DS

- **Screens**: Automatic, Top above bottom, Bottom above top, Side by side (either way round), Hybrid (top or bottom
  large), Top screen only and Bottom screen only. **Top screen only** in the File menu switches quickly.
- **Touch**: your finger is the stylus on the bottom screen.
- **Microphone**: the phone's microphone is never used.
- **DSi mode** needs your own DSi files, and has no save states.

## Picture and sound

**Settings** in the File menu has each console's own options: video filters (Default, Sharp, LCD, CRT and Upscale),
color correction and frame blending on GBA, Game Boy colors with 47 palettes, and frame skip on GBA. Mute is kept per
game.

## Cheats

**Cheats** in the File menu takes GameShark, CodeBreaker and Action Replay codes on GBA, GameShark and Game Genie
codes on Game Boy, and Action Replay codes on DS. They are kept per game, and they are off in Kaizo IronMON runs,
Nuzlockes and RetroAchievements hardcore.

## RetroAchievements

**Achievements** in the File menu: sign in (the password is sent once and never stored), see your progress, and
switch **Hardcore** on. Hardcore turns off cheats, rewind, slow motion, state loads and Time Machine restores.
Achievements are not loaded in Kaizo IronMON runs.

## Cam

**Cam** shows your phone's camera in a bubble over the game (pinch to size it, tap to flip it), or at the top of the
docked tracker in landscape. It is a preview only: nothing is recorded or kept, and it is not sent to a stream.

## Clean view

**Clean view** hides everything but the game, for recording the screen. With the touch pad gone you need a
controller (or touch, on DS). Press Back or tap the corner to leave it.

## Also

- Games that use tilt or a light sensor can use the phone's own sensors.
- BIOS and firmware for GBA, DS and DSi can be imported, if you have your own.
